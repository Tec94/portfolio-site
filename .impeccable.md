## Design context

### Users

The portfolio serves two equally important audiences:

- Hiring teams evaluating Jack Cao's product engineering depth, ownership, and
  ability to ship polished work.
- Freelance clients deciding whether Jack can understand a problem, build the
  right solution, and communicate clearly.

Visitors usually arrive in an exploratory state. They need to understand the
quality of the work quickly before they choose a live project, resume request,
inquiry, or call.

### Brand personality

Precise, kinetic, inventive.

The interface must feel like a carefully engineered instrument. It is
responsive and technically ambitious, but it is not noisy, gimmicky, or harder
to use than a conventional portfolio.

### Aesthetic direction

- Use solid, blue-tinted near-black surfaces with tonal steps and chamfered
  hardware details.
- Keep structural lines within components. Do not use a full-page grid or
  generated texture.
- Cobalt blue is the primary action color.
- Reserve spring green for availability, selection, and the Project Device.
- Use Mozilla Headline for display typography. Use Mozilla Text for body and
  interface copy.
- Treat the Project Device as an original scroll instrument. Keep it partially
  cropped at the archive edge, and step it once when each project crosses the
  viewport center.
- Do not use franchise names, alien silhouettes, projected icons, or project
  glyphs on the Project Device.

### Design principles

1. Put the work first. Animation frames and explains projects, but it does not
   compete with them.
2. Make motion spatially coherent. Scroll direction controls the anticipation,
   dial turn, cover response, and settle.
3. Use spectacle selectively. One strong device transition is more memorable
   than constant glow and movement.
4. Adapt the structure across breakpoints. Mobile uses a purpose-built top bar,
   drawer, stacked archive, and compact edge device.
5. Keep every state honest and accessible. Give keyboard, reduced-motion,
   loading, error, and empty states the same care as the default view.

### Anti-goals

- Generic neon cyberpunk styling, excessive glass panels, gradient text, or
  decorative charts.
- Generated noise, scanlines, full-page grids, or ambient background loops.
- A large selector icon ring outside the watch.
- A separate selector mode that competes with the project archive.
- Equal-card grids on every page.
- Fake contact success, dead links, or controls that only work with a mouse.
