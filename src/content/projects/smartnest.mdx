## Context

Smartnest is a responsive healthcare product-site implementation developed from February through June 2025. I am the sole public repository contributor responsible for the interface, routing, contact flow, SEO work, analytics, and deployment instrumentation.

## Problem

Healthcare product sites have to establish trust quickly while keeping a complex service understandable. The work focused on a clear path from orientation to pricing and contact without turning the page into a dense feature inventory.

## Decisions

### Build a small visual system first

Shared type, spacing, surface, and button decisions gave pricing, navigation, and contact pages a consistent rhythm. That reduced one-off styling as the site expanded.

### Make responsive behavior structural

Mobile navigation, content order, and form layout were designed as first-class states. The implementation did not depend on shrinking the desktop composition until it fit.

### Keep delivery concerns visible

Routing, metadata, image handling, analytics, Speed Insights, sitemap generation, and the contact email path were treated as product work rather than a final deployment checklist.

## Implementation

The repository uses React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Resend, Vercel Analytics, and Speed Insights. Public history records work across themes, pricing, routing, mobile navigation, image optimization, SEO, and contact delivery.

## Outcome

The source records a complete product-site system and its delivery path. The former production domain is not currently available, so this case study does not claim live performance results or current production availability.

## Lessons

Product-site credibility comes from consistency and recovery: predictable navigation, readable pricing, working contact paths, useful metadata, and honest unavailable states matter as much as a polished hero.
