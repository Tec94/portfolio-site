Brian Lovin wrote about an interview exercise he used when hiring design-minded frontend engineers. You build a multi-select dropdown. That's it. The candidates who stood out were the ones who started thinking about things like the order of selected items and keyboard control without anyone asking.

I read that and kind of wanted to see how I'd do. So I built one, and I'm going to write down every decision I hit along the way.
