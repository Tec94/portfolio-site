The Club Cup is the new tournament mode in Runout. You get an eight-player bracket, three rounds, and seven AI rivals, and each rival has a style. Mara is The Closer, so she gets sharper at the end of a rack. Rafe is The Banker and kicks his way out of trouble. Kit is a Gunslinger who fires at everything. I wanted a short loop of a cup run, from the bracket to the 8 going down in the final.

## Recording a whole tournament

I used the same capture setup as the [cues loop](/writing/twenty-six-wild-cues). The app runs in Playwright on a fake clock, the AI plays my side too, and every frame goes straight into ffmpeg. The difference this time is that I didn't record single shots. The script taps Start, then plays the entire cup, from the quarterfinal through the final, without stopping.

Once a second it also reads whatever text is on screen and writes it to a log with a timestamp:

```js
const t = (await text(p)).replace(/\s+/g, ' ');
if (t.slice(0, 200) !== last) {
  last = t.slice(0, 200);
  mark('T ' + last);
}
```

That log turned out to be the most useful part. The game already prints things like "Wrong pocket: the turn passes." and "Foul: scratch. Ball in hand", so finding the good moments in seven minutes of footage was just a text search. The run I used had six wrong pockets and two scratches, and each one came with a timestamp I could cut on.

## Take one lost

The first full run I recorded used seed 99. I won the quarterfinal against Hugo and the semifinal against Rafe, then lost the final to Mara. The result screen says "Lost the rack: the 8 went down on a foul." A loop about winning the cup can't use that, so the script just stops on a loss and logs it, and I ran it again with another seed.

Seed 7 went all the way. That's the run in the loop: Mara goes long and puts a ball in the wrong pocket in the semifinal, Tam scratches in the final, and I get ball in hand and pot the 8. Nothing in it is staged. The AI made those mistakes on its own, and the log told me where they were.

## The edit

The first cut was a HyperFrames film with type and a synthesized sound bed. It got to 7.4 in review, and like the cues film, it didn't match the rest of this site. So I rebuilt it as a silent Remotion loop: real pieces of the app on a plain backdrop, one beat at a time.

The pieces are 3x screenshots from the seed 7 run: the bracket, the results cards and Mara's rival card. The table clip is the end of the final. I get ball in hand, and the 8 goes in. That part plays at half speed while the frame pushes in on the pocket.

The reviews caught one thing I'd gotten wrong. An earlier cut ringed Rafe in the bracket and gave him a beat with a bank shot. But that bank came from the seed 99 run, and in seed 7 Rafe lost his quarterfinal to Tam. He was never my opponent. I cut his beat. Now the loop follows only my side of the bracket: through to the semifinal, Mara next, the final, Champion. After seven rounds it scored 9.
