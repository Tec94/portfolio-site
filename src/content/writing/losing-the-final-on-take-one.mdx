The Club Cup is the new tournament mode in Runout. You get an eight-player bracket, three rounds, and seven AI rivals, and each rival has a style. Mara is The Closer, so she gets sharper at the end of a rack. Rafe is The Banker and kicks his way out of trouble. Kit is a Gunslinger who fires at everything. I wanted a short film showing that the rivals play like people do, which means they also mess up.

## Recording a whole tournament

I used the same capture setup as the [cues film](/writing/twenty-six-wild-cues). The app runs in Playwright on a fake clock, the AI plays my side too, and every frame goes straight into ffmpeg. The difference this time is that I didn't record single shots. The script taps Start, then plays the entire cup, from the quarterfinal through the final, without stopping.

Once a second it also reads whatever text is on screen and writes it to a log with a timestamp:

```js
const t = (await text(p)).replace(/\s+/g, ' ');
if (t.slice(0, 200) !== last) {
  last = t.slice(0, 200);
  mark('T ' + last);
}
```

That log turned out to be the most useful part. The game already prints things like "Wrong pocket: the turn passes." and "Foul: scratch. Ball in hand", so finding the good moments in seven minutes of footage was just a text search. The run in the film had six wrong pockets and two scratches, and each one came with a timestamp I could cut on.

## Take one lost

The first full run I recorded used seed 99. I won the quarterfinal against Hugo and the semifinal against Rafe, then lost the final to Mara. The result screen says "Lost the rack: the 8 went down on a foul." A film about winning the cup can't use that, so the script just stops on a loss and logs it, and I ran it again with another seed.

Seed 7 went all the way. That's the run in the film: Mara goes long and puts a ball in the wrong pocket in the semifinal, Tam scratches in the final, and I get ball in hand and pot the 8. Nothing in it is staged. The AI made those mistakes on its own, and the log told me where they were.

## The edit

Same HyperFrames setup as before: an HTML page, a GSAP timeline, rendered to MP4. I reviewed it in rounds with a fresh reviewer every time. The notes that changed the most were about reading time. The foul captions were up for under half a second, so you couldn't actually read the proof that the rivals make mistakes. The other big one was the win itself, which showed the same results card as the semifinal. I cut the semifinal card, gave each foul more than a second, and rebuilt the Champion card big, in the app's own yellow, over the dimmed result screen.

Those two notes got fixed. The bracket now fills in after the semifinal, and Rafe gets his own beat with a real bank. That bank isn't from seed 7, though. It comes from the seed 99 run, the one I lost, so the film labels it as another cup. Seven rounds in, it sits at 7.4 out of 10.

The sound comes from the same small Python synth as the cues film: a bed, ball clacks, pots and a hit on each cut, mixed to -14 LUFS.
